Firearms and Mounted Unit, Use of Force Event

Posted 9 October 2026

On 8 October our Vice Chair Balbir (pictured) and I attended the Firearms and Mounted Unit Use of Force Event at Tally Ho in Birmingham. It was an evening event that began at around 6pm and consisted of two presentations followed by a tour of the police horse stables.

One of the police horses at the Mounted Unit stables

Although use of force was the central theme, the two presentations were quite different.

The Mounted Unit presentation invited us to consider the historical background of the unit and the various circumstances in which the use of force could be engaged. Meeting the horses and finding out more about them was also included.

Vice Chair Balbir at the event

The Firearms Unit presentation offered attendees the opportunity to take on the virtual position of decision-making police officers in a scenario that involved the possible deployment of a unit. To deploy or not to deploy? — that was the question.

Unfortunately the two hours passed very quickly and we did not manage to complete all of the activities proposed on the agenda. Nonetheless, it was a useful event that offered valuable insight and made particularly clear how quickly decisions need to be made around important matters within a short window of time and opportunity.
